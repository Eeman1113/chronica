## Profession colors

```
gold           ruler
yellow-olive   farmer
green          hunter

brown-gold     woodcutter
mauve-grey     miner

```

Two notes: `;` is heath (scrub), not grass; grass is the `· . , '` family. And `#` means wall inside a town but ruins out in the wilds, told apart by color (tan versus grey) and by whether a settlement is there.
